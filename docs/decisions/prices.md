# Decisions: prices

Oldest first; add new ones at the bottom (format in [README.md](README.md)).

- 2026-10-26 — Outside sources that need an API key are allowed when the key is free to get; paid sources stay out (rule in `docs/sources.md`). Why: every reliable free source of daily prices now needs a free key, and price data is needed to tell whether a stock is already priced in.
- 2026-10-26 — Daily prices come from Stooq (free key), then Tiingo (free key) for what Stooq lacks or refuses, for the market (SPY), the watchlist and the Accumulate candidates only; a pick's price since the quarter funds bought in is set against SPY's over the same days, and 5 points behind or ahead is the line for "lagging" or "ahead". Why: the whole 13F universe is thousands of symbols a day, more than the free sources allow, and the question is only asked of the stocks the app recommends or you watch; SPY is the plainest free stand-in for the market; 5 points is about a quarter's typical spread between a stock and the index, to be tuned once a few quarters of picks exist.
- 2026-11-01 — Polygon (free key) is the first fallback for daily prices, between Stooq and Tiingo, asked one symbol at a time and paced to its free plan's 5 requests a minute; its grouped daily endpoint (the whole market's bars for one day in one request) is noted but not used. Why: of the free sources on the public-apis Finance list it is the only one with no monthly symbol cap, 300 symbols an hour beats Tiingo's 50, and a wait of under a minute is pacing to a published limit, not a retry; the whole-universe fetch would reverse the Oct 26 decision to price only the picks and the watchlist, which is a separate call.
