Why this page shows fund counts, not shares

Data check, Q2 2026

In the source’s June 2026 figures, the shares funds hold in each of the seven largest stocks jump by 46% to 117%, while the dollar value of those holdings moves 2% to 35%. Share totals that grow far faster than value point to a reporting error in at least one filing, so the shares are left out here. Method

Funds holding counts the 13F filings that list each stock for the quarter end, as compiled by 13f.info from SEC 13F-HR filings. A manager can file more than one report, so read counts as close, not exact.

The watchlist median is the middle change across these eight stocks for the same period. The finished app should compare with the change in the total number of 13F filers instead.

13F covers managers with $100M or more in US-listed securities, and long positions only. Late filings can still add a few funds to the latest quarter.
