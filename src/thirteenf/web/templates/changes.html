Method

Each stock is compared with itself one period earlier. Change against the tide is its percentage change in funds holding minus the percentage change in all 13F filers: {{ fmt.count(summary.filers) }} managers filed holdings for {{ selected.label }}, {{ fmt.pct(summary.filers_pct) }} on the period before. Ranking by change in funds uses the plain difference in the number of funds instead.

Only stocks held by at least {{ fmt.count(minimum) }} funds at both dates are ranked, so a stock new to 13F filings, or one that left them, does not top a list on small counts. When a company changed its CUSIP, its funds are counted across both CUSIPs.

The change in shares held is shown only when the share total passes the data checks: reports whose value per share is more than 3 times off the median are left out, and shares must move in line with value once the price per share is allowed for.

13F covers managers with $100M or more in US-listed securities, and long positions only. Funds holding counts managers, so a manager that files late can still change the latest period.
