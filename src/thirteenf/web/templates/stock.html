Method

Funds holding counts the managers whose 13F-HR report for each quarter end lists shares of {{ sec.name }}, from the SEC’s Form 13F data sets. Each manager counts once: a restatement replaces its report, and a new-holdings amendment adds to it. Options and bond principal are left out.

Shares and value come from the same rows. A report whose value per share is more than 3 times above or below the median across all funds holding the stock is left out of the share total and listed under Data check. Reports from managers that still give value in thousands of dollars are kept, with their value scaled. Value moves with the share price, so a lower value does not by itself mean funds sold.

The market median is the middle change in funds holding across every stock held by at least 100 funds in both periods{% if latest_period.market_median_stocks %} ({{ fmt.count(latest_period.market_median_stocks) }} stocks in {{ latest_period.label }}){% endif %}. All 13F filers is the change in the number of managers filing holdings. Every Dec 31 both jump, because newly large managers file their first 13F, so compare a stock with them rather than with zero.

13F covers managers with $100M or more in US-listed securities, and long positions only. Filings made after {{ fmt.day(latest_period.data_through) }} arrive in the SEC’s next data set, so late filers can still add a few funds to the latest quarter.
