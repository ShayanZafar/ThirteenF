Method

Positions come from {{ book.name }}’s 13F-HR report for {{ book_label }} in the SEC’s Form 13F data sets, with amendments applied: a restatement replaces the report, and a new-holdings amendment adds to it. Share positions only; options and bond principal are left out, as are shorts and cash, which 13F does not cover.

Weight in book is a position’s value as a share of the reported 13F portfolio. Shares changed compares with the {{ prev_label }} report, counting a company’s change of CUSIP as the same stock; net buying is shares changed × the {{ fmt.short_day(book.period) }} price, the median value per share across all funds holding the stock.

A report whose value per share is more than 3 times off the median is marked Check, and its value is left out of the portfolio total.
